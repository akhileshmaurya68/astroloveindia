How to Get Rid of Manglik Dosh: Cancellation Rules, Remedies, Temples & Poojas

Being Manglik is one of the most feared and most misunderstood ideas in Indian marriage matching. This guide explains what it really is, when it cancels by itself, and which practical, ritual and temple remedies people follow, with an honest view of what each can and cannot do.

1. What Is Manglik Dosh (Mangal Dosh)?

Manglik dosh, also called Kuja dosh or Bhauma dosh, arises when Mars (Mangal) sits in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th house of the birth chart. Many traditions skip the 2nd house, and North and South Indian schools differ, so always ask which rule your astrologer uses. The check is made from the Lagna and, in stricter methods, also from the Moon and Venus.

Mars is the planet of heat, drive and anger. In the houses of self, home, partnership, longevity and bed pleasures, classical texts say its energy can bring quarrels, ego clashes, delays in marriage or friction with in-laws. Intensity is graded: 7th and 8th house Mars is the most intense, 1st and 4th are moderate, and 2nd and 12th are mild.

1st house

Strong ego, dominating nature, impatience.

4th house

Restless home, tension with mother or in-laws.

7th house

Heated partnership, strong will on both sides.

8th house

Sudden changes, in-law and health stress.

12th house

Bedroom friction, overspending, distance.

2nd house

Harsh speech, family-money disagreements.

Reality check: roughly 40% or more of all charts show Mars in one of these houses, so a large part of the population is "Manglik". No research shows Manglik couples are less happy than others. Treat it as a chart factor to be understood, never as a verdict.

2. Does Manglik Dosh Go Away After a Certain Age?

A popular tradition says the dosh weakens or ends after age 28, the age by which Mars is said to mature. Some regional texts say 27, others 28 or 32. It is best read as a rule of thumb: a person with an intense 7th-house Mars may still need to apply the cancellation rules in the next section, while a mild 2nd or 12th-house Mars rarely needs any remedy at all. The Mars dasha and Mars transits over the natal Mars matter more than the calendar age. A late marriage by itself neither guarantees nor removes anything.

3. Planetary Positions That Cancel Manglik Dosh Automatically

Classical Jyotish describes Manglik Bhanga, or cancellation. A chart with any of these is considered safe or greatly reduced:

  • Mars is conjunct the Moon, Jupiter or Mercury, or aspected by benefics.
  • Both partners are Manglik, so the heat is matched (the most commonly used rule).
  • The 7th lord and Venus are strong, and Mars is strong in Navamsa (D9).
  • Saturn also sitting in the same Manglik houses balances Mars in many matching methods.
  • A Manglik from the Lagna but not from the Moon is treated as mild.

Cancellation lists vary between texts, so confirm with a full chart reading, not a free online calculator.

4. Practical Tips to Nullify Manglik Dosh

Mars gives trouble through temper and impatience, so the most effective remedies are behavioural. These match the "Graha habits" approach used across this site:

Burn the heat

20-30 minutes of vigorous exercise or sport, ideally on Tuesdays. Mars needs an outlet.

10-minute pause rule

Never reply in anger. Wait ten minutes, then talk.

Meet before marrying

Know your partner's temper, money habits and family before fixing the date.

Match the partner

Marry another Manglik, or a person with strong Jupiter and Venus.

Counselling

Premarital or couple counselling works better than any gemstone for conflicts.

Cool the body

Reduce excess spice, alcohol and late-night screen use on Mars days.

Gemstone caution: red coral strengthens Mars, which can worsen the problem in some charts. Never wear it without a complete chart review.

5. Rituals to Cancel Manglik Dosh

Common rituals for both partners

  • Tuesday fast (Mangal Vrat): eat once, keep it sattvic, worship Lord Hanuman.
  • Hanuman Chalisa every Tuesday and Saturday (11 times on Tuesday is popular).
  • Mangal beej mantra: "Om Kram Krim Kraum Sah Bhaumaya Namah", 108 times daily, or 10,000 repetitions in the classical count.
  • Daan on Tuesday: red lentils (masoor dal), jaggery, red cloth or sweets, given to the needy.

For the groom after marriage

  • Continue the Tuesday Hanuman worship and mantra with his wife joining at least once a month.
  • Offer sindoor and jasmine oil to Hanuman, and serve his in-laws and elders respectfully.
  • Donate blood or help in a healthcare cause. Traditionally linked with Mars (blood).
  • Perform a Mangal Shanti puja on the first wedding anniversary.

For the bride after marriage

  • Mangala Gauri Vrat on Tuesdays of the Shravan month, usually for the first five years of marriage.
  • Worship Goddess Parvati (Gauri) with red flowers, vermilion and sweets, and read Mangal Chandika Stotra.
  • Offer a Tuesday prayer to Hanuman for her husband's long life and calm.
  • Keep red bangles and vermilion with devotion as an act of auspicious Mars energy, not as pressure.

6. Temples to Visit for Manglik Dosh

Mangalnath Temple, Ujjain

Traditionally the birthplace of Mars. The best-known site for Manglik dosh puja (Bhaat Puja), busiest on Tuesdays.

Vaitheeswaran Koil, Tamil Nadu

Navagraha temple for Mars (Angaraka); also known for healing prayers.

Mangal Graha Mandir, Amalner

A Mars temple in Maharashtra's Jalgaon district, popular on Angaraki Chaturthi and Tuesdays.

Palani & Thiruchendur Murugan

Murugan (Kartikeya) is the deity of Mars in the South; Tuesday abhishekam is common.

Sankat Mochan Hanuman, Varanasi

Hanuman worship pacifies Mars energy. Many families offer Tuesday prayers here.

Any local Hanuman temple

Regular Tuesday visits near home matter more than one distant pilgrimage.

Check opening rules and puja fees directly with the temple trust. Do not pay agents who promise "guaranteed" results.

7. Poojas and Havans to Cancel Manglik Dosh

  • Mangal Dosh Nivaran / Bhaat Puja (Ujjain): offering rice to Mars, usually performed on a Tuesday.
  • Mangal Shanti Havan: fire ritual with khadira (khair wood) samidha and Mars mantras, ideally on the Mars hora of a Tuesday.
  • Kumbh Vivah: a symbolic marriage before the real wedding (see next section).
  • Rin Mochan Mangal Stotra paath for debts and conflict.
  • Navagraha Shanti puja when other afflictions exist along with Mars.

Choose an experienced pandit, use a muhurta given for your own chart, and keep expenses reasonable. A simple home havan done with sincerity is as valid as a costly one.

8. Is Marrying a Tree (Kumbh Vivah) a Solution?

In Kumbh Vivah, a Manglik person is ritually wed to a Peepal or banana tree, a clay pot (kumbh) or a Vishnu idol before the real marriage. The tree or idol "absorbs" the dosh. For men, the ritual often uses the Arka (Madar) plant. It is a symbolic ritual, not a legal marriage.

Honest view: followers see it as a powerful ritual of surrender, and it is widely practised. Critics, including many astrologers, point out that no scientific evidence supports it. If it gives you or your family peace of mind and you can afford it, there is no harm in doing it. But it should not replace matching charts carefully, meeting the partner, and building maturity. Avoid pressure, large fees and fear selling.

9. Celebrity Couples and Manglik Dosh

Verified birth details of public figures are rarely available, so most "celebrity Manglik lists" online are guesswork. The best-known case discussed by Indian media is Aishwarya Rai and Abhishek Bachchan, who married on 20 April 2007 and have a daughter, Aaradhya, born in 2011. Before the wedding, media reports said Aishwarya was Manglik and had undergone Kumbh Vivah with trees; the family has publicly ridiculed the tree story, and her Manglik status itself remains a media report, not a confirmed fact.

The lesson is the same for everyone: the media attention in 2007 turned "Manglik" into a household word, yet no astrological label can predict how any marriage turns out. We deliberately do not name other couples without reliable public confirmation, and we do not repeat gossip about anyone's personal life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a Manglik marry a non-Manglik?

Yes, if the Manglik dosh is mild or cancelled, or if remedies and a strong 7th house are present. Traditionally, matching is advised, but a full chart review is better than a yes/no rule.

Is Manglik dosh real?

It is a belief within Vedic astrology and has no scientific proof. Treat it as a symbol for temper and impatience, which are real relationship challenges.

Which day is best for Manglik dosh remedies?

Tuesday is the day of Mars, especially during the Mars hora (the first planetary hour after sunrise on Tuesdays).

Does Kumbh Vivah have to be done before marriage?

Yes, it is traditionally performed before the real wedding, preferably on an auspicious date chosen by a priest.

Can Manglik dosh cause divorce?

Classical texts link it to friction, but a marriage depends far more on communication, respect and compatibility than on one planet.
